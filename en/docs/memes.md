Christopher Chong's AI-generated satire (copy: 「有些东西是避无可避的 #AI #人工智能」). In a future museum the guide (an AI voice) says the figure ahead is a precious purely human employee. A man types at an old computer inside a glass case. "What is he doing?" "Making a PowerPoint by hand." "Doesn't he use AI?" "Handmade slides are a national intangible heritage." AI-generated tells: the mouth does not match, the hand fuses with the phone, and the voiceover is synthetic. Music: none, only room tone. Interview takeaway: routine knowledge work such as slides and documents is automated first. An engineer's value shifts toward judgment and defining the problem.
