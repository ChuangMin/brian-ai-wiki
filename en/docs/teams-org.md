### [Peter Sellis: cut collaboration cost with a creed plus a decision structure](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1728245575311571)

Lower a team's collaboration cost with a short creed and clear decision rights

A September clip from Lenny's Podcast, arranged by Tenten. Sellis was Snapchat's first PM and Discord's head of product (the copy says he becomes X Head of Product on 10/26). His point: collaboration has a hidden cost, and a team moves at the speed of its slowest node. To go fast you need (1) an ideology: compress the strategy into a short line people can repeat, and (2) a decision structure: say in advance who can decide what, and who is the single owner. The bar for a PM is to make the decision the founder would have made six months to a year or two earlier, which requires delegated trust. "Like a terrorist organization" is a metaphor he himself says should be used carefully.
